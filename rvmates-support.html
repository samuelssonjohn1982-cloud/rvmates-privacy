RVMates Support

Help & frequently asked questions

RVMates helps motorhome and caravan owners stay connected with their travel group, share their live location, and discover other campers nearby.

Frequently asked questions

  • How do I sign in? RVMates uses passwordless sign-in: enter your email address and we'll send you a one-time code. No password to remember.
  • How do I create or join a group? Open the Friends tab, create a group to get an invite code, or enter a code you received to join an existing group.
  • How do I stop sharing my location? Go to the Map or Profile tab and turn off location sharing at any time.
  • How do I leave or delete a group? Open the Friends tab — any member can leave the group, and the creator can delete it entirely.
  • How do I delete my account? Go to Profile → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, location data, and messages.
